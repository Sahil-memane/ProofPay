1. Getting Started

ProofPay has two engines, Escrow PREVENT and Revenue RECOVER, that share one deterministic trust layer.

1

Client, Freelancer, Merchant, or Risk Officer. Each sees only the actions it is allowed to take.
2

Frequently asked

Is this connected to real money?

No. The workspace runs against the PayPal Sandbox, as shown by the Sandbox badge in the header. No real funds move.

Who decides whether a payout is released?

The AI proposes, but a deterministic policy engine (Mandate Guard) decides. Payouts over the limit are routed to a human in the Approvals Queue.

Can records be edited after the fact?

No. Each audit event includes the hash of the previous one, so changing any record breaks the chain and is detectable.

Where do I change my PayPal recipient email?

Go to Settings → Profile. Mandate Guard requires a verified PayPal email before any payout.

2. Escrow PREVENT Guide

Dual approval & AI verification

Protects clients and freelancers from non-payment, scope creep, and delivery disputes.

1
Intake & AI scoping. Describe the engagement. The Scoping Agent turns it into milestones with testable acceptance criteria.
2
Dual approval. The platform computes a SHA-256 Plan Hash of all milestones, amounts, and criteria. Client and Freelancer must both sign that exact hash before funding unlocks.
3
Fund escrow. The client pays through PayPal Checkout (Orders v2 create and capture). Funds are held in the escrow pool.
4
Submit work. The freelancer provides a GitHub PR URL, repository, staging URL, evidence files, and notes.
5
AI verification. The Verification Agent scores the deliverable from 0.0 to 1.0 against the criteria and picks a route (below).
6
Mandate Guard & disbursement. If policy allows, the payout is sent to the freelancer's verified PayPal account via the Payouts API. Otherwise it goes to the Approvals Queue.
Plan hash example: 9f2c…e41a (illustrative). If the plan is changed outside the approval flow, hashes no longer match and the API returns 409 Conflict.

< 0.60AUTO_REJECTDeliverable returned to the freelancer.

3. Revenue RECOVER Guide

3-way reconciliation

Recovers failed subscription charges and overdue invoices without damaging the customer relationship.

How 3-way reconciliation stops wrongful dunning

PAID_ELSEWHERE outreach permanently halted

Example: a $450.00 card charge failed, but a matching $450.00 wire was found on the bank statement. The case is closed as paid elsewhere and no reminder is ever sent.

Recovery steps

1
Detect. Failures and overdue invoices arrive via webhook or CSV import.
2
Diagnose. The AI classifies the cause: EXPIRED_CARD, INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS, BANK_AUTH_FAILED, or GATEWAY_ERROR.
3
Contact ladder. Stage 1 Soft Update Link, Stage 2 Firm Warning, Stage 3 Executive Pause.
4
Quiet hours. Messages and retries are suspended from 21:00 to 08:00 in the customer's local time.
5
Customer portal. The customer pays in one click through PayPal, or updates their card.
6
Resolve. The PayPal webhook confirms payment, the case becomes PAID, and the ticket closes.
